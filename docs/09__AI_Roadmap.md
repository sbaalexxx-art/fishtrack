# AIFishMap Artificial Intelligence Roadmap

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Artificial Intelligence strategy of AIFishMap.

Artificial Intelligence is designed to assist anglers by analysing data and providing intelligent recommendations.

AI is a decision support system.

The angler always makes the final decision.

---

# Vision

AIFishMap AI combines:

- Hydrological data
- Weather
- Historical catches
- Community reports
- Seasonality
- Water trends

to generate useful fishing insights.

---

# AI Principles

Artificial Intelligence must be:

- Helpful
- Explainable
- Reliable
- Transparent

Predictions should always be supported by real data whenever possible.

---

# AI Modules

## Fishing Score

Generates a score between 0 and 100 indicating expected fishing conditions.

Factors include:

- Water level
- Water trend
- Weather
- Wind
- Pressure
- Moon phase
- Season
- Historical catches
- Community activity

---

## Smart Insights

Generate simple explanations.

Example:

"The river is rising slowly and pressure is stable. Conditions are improving."

---

## Activity Prediction

Predict:

- Low activity
- Medium activity
- High activity

for selected locations.

---

## Species Prediction

Future feature.

Estimate the probability of catching specific species based on:

- Location
- Season
- Weather
- Historical records

---

## Smart Notifications

Examples:

- Excellent fishing conditions tomorrow.
- Sudden water increase.
- Strong wind warning.
- Increased community activity.

---

# AI Data Sources

Artificial Intelligence receives processed information from:

- Supabase
- Backend
- Official providers
- Community reports

AI never communicates directly with external providers.

---

# Machine Learning

Machine Learning will improve predictions over time.

The more validated data available, the better the recommendations.

---

# Future AI Features

Possible future modules:

- Best fishing time
- Best bait suggestion
- Session planner
- Route recommendation
- Personal fishing assistant
- Personalised learning based on user history

---

# Privacy

AI analyses anonymised data whenever possible.

Personal information must never be exposed.

All AI processing must comply with applicable privacy regulations.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Make AIFishMap the smartest freshwater fishing assistant in Europe.

---

# Official Rule

Artificial Intelligence supports anglers.

It never replaces their knowledge or experience.
